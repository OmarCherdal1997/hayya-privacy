Hayya Prayer times, qibla and adhkar

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Hayya works on your phone. It uses your location only to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction, and that location never leaves your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

This policy explains how the Hayya app for Android and iOS ("Hayya", "the app"), published by [YOUR NAME], handles information. If you have a question, contact us at [YOUR EMAIL].

Information the app uses

  • Location. If you tap "Use my location", Hayya asks for permission to read your device's location. It uses it only to calculate prayer times and the qibla bearing on your phone. Your coordinates are saved on your device so the app works next time, and they are not sent to us or to anyone else. You can choose a city or type coordinates instead, and you can turn off location permission at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Settings and adhkar progress. Your calculation method, reminder choices, theme and daily adhkar counts are stored only on your device.
  • Compass sensor. The qibla screen reads your phone's compass while it is open. These readings are used on the spot and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications. Prayer and adhkar reminders are scheduled by your phone's own system. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Information we do not collect

Hayya has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or any other personal details. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your data.

Fonts downloaded from Google

To display Arabic and Latin text, Hayya downloads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time they are needed. Like any connection to a web server, this request includes your device's IP address and basic technical information, which Google receives under the Google Privacy Policy. No location, settings or other app data is included in these requests.

Google Play and Apple App Store

When you download Hayya, Google or Apple may collect information under their own privacy policies, for example to process the download or to send us anonymous crash and install statistics through their developer consoles. We do not receive information that identifies you personally from these reports.

Data retention and deletion

All information the app keeps is stored on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling Hayya. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Your data stays in Hayya's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Fonts are downloaded over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Children

Hayya is suitable for a general audience. Since it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
